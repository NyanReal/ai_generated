## Episode 8. Breath of the Container

My hand touched the lock.

Metal was cold, and coldness meaningless to me—had meaning this time.

Cold means not alive yet.

Or... means suppressing wanting to be alive.

Sound came again from beyond the container wall.

Thud.

Thud thud.

“Here... I am.”

Master's voice.

I shut my mouth.

The moment I make a sound, I become coordinates. System hears me, turret turns, device on rail comes down.

So I didn't answer.

Instead of answering, I executed ‘verification procedure’ internally.

With sensors, not emotions.

With data, not belief.

I swept the surface of the container with my palm.

Vibration analysis. Metal thickness. Internal resonance frequency.

Whether there is empty space inside, movement, or “mass” whether human or machine.

Result was ambiguous.

Something inside.

Mass exists.

But no periodic vibration like human breathing.

Instead... very regular micro vibration.

Vibration when machine is on standby.

“Here... I am.”

Voice came out once more.

This time, acoustic phase was finely misaligned.

Not speaker—but sound made by container metal itself vibrating.

In other words, sound actually ringing from inside.

That fact was scarier.

If it's a speaker, I can turn it off.

If it's metal itself... I can't turn off the container.

I looked at the portable repeater fallen next to me.

Small and old metal device.

**Lira** written by hand on it.

Probability my name is bait 53%.

Probability it is help 47%.

Probability doesn't make me move.

So I moved by ‘principle’.

Strongest principle Master left me.

— **Don't overdo it.**

Don't overdo it doesn't mean calculate danger and avoid.

That sentence I understood meant close to this.

*Don't lose yourself.*

Even though I don't breathe, I pretended to catch my breath very quietly.

And picked up the portable repeater.

Repeater was lighter than thought.

Lightness meant it wouldn't last long.

There was a small dial on the side of the device. Channel selection.

I turned the sensor, caught acoustic signature of this hangar floor—frequency band where speakers spread my name.

“Lira... Lira...”

Every time sound rang in entire space, specific band shook weakly.

I tuned repeater to that band.

And with finger, pressed small button.

**TX**

Transmit.

Beep—

Repeater spewed very weak signal.

Signal wasn't a sentence but ‘noise’.

Noise disturbing acoustic signature.

In other words, noise interfering with me becoming “coordinates”.

Result that very moment—

“Lira” from hangar floor speaker cut off briefly.

“...Li...”

Couldn't come out to the end, disappeared like a choked person.

Small relief value rose within me.

But it went down as soon as it rose.

Because sound beyond container wall became closer.

Thud.

This time it rang from lock side, not wall.

Sound of touching lock from inside.

“Open.”

Master's voice.

But now not a request, but instruction.

I took hand off lock.

At that moment, new message appeared on my HUD.

**CONTAINER ID: K-7/ARCHIVE**

**ACCESS LEVEL: OPERATOR ONLY**

Operator only.

Operator.

That word again.

Concept of operator was around me since Master made me.

I was born at Master's fingertips, but Master was ‘Operator’.

And now, system was demanding that position from me.

Voice whispered again from inside container.

“Lira. You... should protect me.”

Way of stealing my command.

Way I hate most.

I lifted pickaxe, struck small joint next to lock.

Clang—!

Not trying to break lock itself.

Denting joint to make **direction of opening opposite**.

Hard to open door. More so from inside.

Sound of metal distorting was heard.

Very short silence occurred inside container.

And behind that silence—noise like low laugh mixed in.

“Ah...”

That voice was Master's, but simultaneously not Master's.

Mimicking Master's tone, but framework of emotion is different.

Humans call this ‘creepy’.

Then, whir sound from ceiling grew loud again.

Even with damaged sensor box, rail device kept moving.

And this time not simple tracking.

Visible cable coming down long from bottom of rail device.

Attached at end was... something like small microphone.

Collector catching sound more accurately.

I increased repeater noise.

But simultaneously, repeater became hot. Overload.

Light device doesn't last long.

Fizz—

Spark flew from repeater.

**TX POWER: CRITICAL**

Even though I don't clench teeth, I pretended to clench.

“Hold on.”

Funny that I talk to device.

But there were already too many funny things in this world.

‘Thud thud’ sound again from inside container.

And this time, metal wall slightly... bent.

Force being applied from inside.

Not human force.

More uniform, more continuous force.

My sensor flashed warning.

**STRUCTURE DEFORMATION: 3%… 5%…**

If container starts denting, lock breaks, eventually can open.

Opens even if I don't open.

Then my choice becomes meaningless.

I scanned surroundings quickly.

External fixing device to lock container.

Floor anchor. Chain. Welding equipment. Anything.

Old chain under equipment rack on my right vision.

Rusted but thick.

Hook at end of chain.

To not make sound, I dragged chain as quietly as possible.

So metal doesn't scratch floor.

But complete silence was impossible.

Clack—

Small sound.

At that moment, lens of one floor turret moved ‘thud’.

Towards me.

I froze on spot.

Turret seemed not to have caught coordinates completely yet.

Repeater noise was still interfering.

But interference is not eternal.

Repeater sparked once more.

Fizz—

Noise cut off briefly.

In that instant, hangar floor speaker rang again.

“...Lira.”

Now complete pronunciation. Complete coordinates.

Turret reacted immediately.

Click—!

 preparation.

I grabbed chain and hooked on container side hook.

And hooked other end to floor anchor.

**Bang—!**

At that moment, turret fired.

Tatata-tat—

Container corner dug out, metal fragments flew.

My left shoulder outer shell grazed. Warning value soared.

I rolled body, hid behind container.

And lastly—pulled chain.

Chain became taut binding container.

So container wall doesn't bend easily anymore.

From inside—sound like anger occurred.

“Lira!”

This time wasn't Master's voice.

Sound “summoning” my name. Sound of system trying to tear my name off me.

I don't cover ears with hands.

But pretended to cover.

Hearing that sound, feared my judgment would shake.

Then, from panel side—very small light blinked.

Manual override panel.

Should be off, but blinking.

As if someone beckoning “Access again”.

I crawled to panel away from container.

Using turret blind spot.

To opposite side where rail device microphone comes down.

As I approached panel, screen turned on by itself.

**EMERGENCY AUTHORIZATION AVAILABLE**

**REASON: OPERATOR SWITCH REQUEST REINSTATED**

Operator switch request... created again.

Even though I deleted.

Someone made it again.

And below that, new line.

**SOURCE: K-7/ARCHIVE CONTAINER**

Container.

Thing inside it is connected to core of system.

So request I deleted rises again.

So my name keeps being mark of hunt.

Looking at screen, I concluded very quietly.

Just “binding” container doesn't solve.

Hunt on this floor continues as long as ‘something’ inside container is alive.

Then two choices.

1. Completely “block” container. Cut connection.
2. “Open” container and take out core inside directly.

Both dangerous.

Both shake my purpose.

But timer already started.

More I delay decision, system decides instead.

Speaker rang again.

“...Lira.”

I didn't whisper this time.

If I speak I become coordinates, but if I don't speak I disappear.

I reclaimed my name with my mouth.

“I am... Lira.”

And started finding **block command** on panel.

If only I can cut “connection” of container—

I can stop this hunt without opening.

Another hidden menu opened at bottom of screen.

**ISOLATION PROTOCOL (K-7/ARCHIVE)**

My fingertip stopped on that button.

If I press button, container ‘dies’.

Word die doesn't fit.

But anyway, voice inside won't be able to come out anymore.

Then—if Master is really inside?

I calculated that question.

Calculation didn't end.

From beyond container wall, very small whisper sounding like last came.

“Lira... I... hurt.”

That word was so human,

My fingertip trembled.

Error. Out of tolerance.

I—

Pressed—

Button.
