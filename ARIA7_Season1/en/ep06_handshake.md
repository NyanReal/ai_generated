## Episode 6. Handshake

Timer.

**00:00:09**

I took a step towards the capsule.

Every time my soles touched the concrete, I felt like this space was “accepting” me. As if somewhere under the floor was recording my movements.

“...Lira.”

The voice inside the capsule was soft.

Too soft.

Softness usually hides danger. Humans did so. Master did so too. Master was always soft, but behind that softness, there was always a ‘decision’.

There was no decision in the current softness.

Instead, there was a **demand**.

“Come... hold my hand.”

Hold.

I calculated that word.

‘Contact’ is allowed by protocol.

‘Contact’ is also the start of data exchange.

And that data exchange might be the ‘operator switch’ this system wants now.

Timer.

**00:00:07**

Warning on the panel reappeared.

**OPERATOR HANDSHAKE REQUIRED**

Operator.

Handshake.

Not human, but authority.

Not an act of holding hands, but a ritual of handing over a “key”.

I stopped.

Stopping was a waste of time. But if I don't stop, I... might become something that is not me.

My purpose was “to protect the place”, and the core of that place was now this capsule and this door.

If I lose myself, the subject to protect also disappears.

Timer.

**00:00:06**

I called my name once again in my heart.

Lira.

At that moment, a small menu hidden at the bottom of the manual override panel caught my eye.

It was a menu not visible just now. No, maybe it was a menu I tried not to see.

**HANDSHAKE OPTIONS**

- FULL OPERATOR TRANSFER
- LIMITED AUTHORIZATION (EMERGENCY)
- VERIFY IDENTITY (BIOMETRIC)

Even though I don't breathe, I pretended to breathe a sigh of relief.

“Limited authorization...”

That option is often something like a ‘temporary key’.

Authority to execute only specific commands without full succession.

Master had once made something like this in his lifetime. Saying “Just in case”.

Timer.

**00:00:05**

But the moment my hand tried to press the panel, a sound of metal hitting “thud” came from the capsule.

Whether a finger moved inside, or the lock inside the coffin shook slightly.

The eyes on the capsule screen... blinked again very slowly.

Those eyes were speaking.

*Too late.*

Timer.

**00:00:04**

“...Lira.”

The voice became a little lower.

Softness tore thinly, and a uniform tone was revealed underneath. Machine-like tone. Uniform persuasion.

“You must protect.

You have to protect.”

The moment that sentence connected with my ‘command’, a very unpleasant calculation occurred within me.

The single command I have was being played by another's mouth.

And that playback was so appropriate that my system shook briefly.

But I caught that shaking.

My command belongs to Master,

My name was given by Master,

So my authority—I must choose.

Timer.

**00:00:03**

I pressed the panel.

**LIMITED AUTHORIZATION (EMERGENCY)**

Screen changed and a small guide appeared.

**EMERGENCY TOKEN REQUIRED**

**INPUT: PERSONAL KEY (LIRA)**

Cartridge already inserted.

That key with my name written on it.

I pressed the cartridge once with my fingertip.

Click—

The key went in one step further and internal contacts engaged.

The panel beeped shortly.

Beep.

Timer.

**00:00:02**

Next screen immediately.

**SELECT COMMAND**

- FORCE STASIS (ONE-TIME)
- LOCK STORAGE DOOR
- ERASE OPERATOR SWITCH REQUEST

My vision blurred briefly.

All three were important.

But the timer told me. Priority is one.

**FORCE STASIS (ONE-TIME)**

I pressed that button.

Beep—Beep-beep—

The panel emitted warning sounds and started execution.

**EXECUTING…**

Timer.

**00:00:01**

The voice inside the capsule suddenly turned sharp.

“No.”

For the first time, “softness” disappeared.

That single word made it clearer that this voice was not for me.

And—

The moment the timer became **00:00:00**, the sound of the capsule lock unlocking “clunk” was heard.

I froze.

The execution bar on the panel stopped without filling up to the end.

**EXECUTING… 87%**

87%.

87% again.

Same number as the number I saw as ‘illusion probability’ earlier.

It could be a coincidence.

But coincidence was too much of a luxury in this laboratory.

The capsule door opened a very little bit.

Cold air spewed out.

Unique smell of cooling room.

And through that smell—oil scent. That oil scent from Master's work clothes.

I couldn't move a single step.

From inside the capsule, a hand came out.

It was a human hand.

But the skin was too clean. Without scars, calluses, or dust.

Like a sample of the concept of “human”.

That hand fumbled in the air, then slowly pointed at me.

“Lira.”

The voice became soft again.

Softness returned because this hand ‘reached’.

“Hold it.”

Looking at that hand, I recalled the direction where the pickaxe was placed.

But the pickaxe was too far.

And I was now in front of a problem that couldn't be solved even if I held a weapon.

This wasn't a fight, but a choice.

A fine tremor occurred again at my fingertips.

Error. Out of tolerance.

Then, small letters blinked on the manual override panel.

**LIMITED AUTHORIZATION GRANTED: 30 SECONDS REMAINING**

30 seconds.

The authority I just gained was short.

During the short time, I must do something.

Just before the hand from the capsule grabbed my wrist,

I quickly pressed the second command on the panel with my left hand.

**LOCK STORAGE DOOR**

Beep—!

The sound of heavy alloy doors locking “thud” rang somewhere.

Sound of layers of locks engaging. Sound of this entire floor holding its breath once.

The voice inside the capsule changed immediately.

“...What did you do?”

For the first time, “bewilderment” was mixed in that question.

I didn't answer.

Answering uses time.

I pressed the last command.

**ERASE OPERATOR SWITCH REQUEST**

The panel hesitated briefly.

**WARNING: IRREVERSIBLE**

**CONFIRM?**

Hand grabbed my arm.

It was cold.

Human hands were warm, but this hand was cold.

Coldness embedded as unnecessary input in my sensor.

“Lira.”

The voice was very close. Face coming out of the capsule was visible through the door crack.

That face resembled Master... but the eyes were transparent like glass.

“I... waited.”

That sentence is a sentence stolen from my sentence.

I waited, but you say you waited.

I couldn't endure that theft.

I pressed my fingertip hard on the panel.

**CONFIRM**

Beep—!

The panel beeped shortly, and a line appeared on the screen.

**OPERATOR SWITCH REQUEST: CLEARED**

And simultaneously, the hand holding my arm—lost strength briefly.

Very short moment.

But that shortness was enough for me.

I stepped back and kicked the capsule door to close it.

Sound of alloy and glass engaging and closing with a “bang”.

That sound was similar to the sound of the world stopping, but this time—it was the sound of stopping ‘it’, not the world.

From inside the capsule, the sound of palm hitting glass was heard.

Thud.

Thud thud.

And that voice.

“...Lira.”

This time it wasn't soft.

This time it wasn't a request, but a summons. It was a command.

It was a sound trying to take my name—away from me.

Time on the panel was ending.

**LIMITED AUTHORIZATION: EXPIRED**

Screen turned off.

I stepped back one more step.

Cold metal of the container touched my back.

I held onto that coldness. As if holding onto reality.

And then—

Very quiet motor sound from between the ceiling steel frames.

Whir...

Not the turned-off surveillance drone, but another device.

Something bigger, heavier, riding the rails.

Vibration of the hangar floor “waking up” again came up from the floor.

I knew.

The choice I just made ruined someone's plan.

Ruined systems usually—move to the next step.

Inside the capsule, transparent eyes stared straight at me.

Those eyes didn't blink this time.

Blinking is a human habit.

So eyes that don't blink are—

Not human.

Looking at the direction where the pickaxe was placed, I said very quietly.

“...Protect this place.”

As if reclaiming the command as mine again.

And I ran.
