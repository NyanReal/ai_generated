## Episode 18. Core (Season 1 Finale)

In deepest part of core, there was one more door.

Doors until now were alloy.
This door was... glass.
Glass, but thick and dark.
Inside is not visible.
‘Glass made not to see’ is like mirror.

Label written above door.

**SEED VAULT**

Seed vault.
Place where remote piloting seeds are stored.
In other words, place where ‘mimicry of Master’ is born.

Mira whispered next to me.

“Here... can we end it?”

I wasn't sure.
But even without certainty, choice is possible.

Panel next to door displayed question again.

**WHAT ARE YOU?**

I raised fingertip.
Not question demanding correct answer,
Question forcing ‘Self-introduction’.

Laboratory classifies you through introduction.
Once classified, controls.

I looked at Mira.
Mira nodded.
Meant “Let's write together”.

I input.

**WE ARE LIRA AND MIRA.**
**WE ARE NOT OPERATORS.**
**WE ARE GUARDIANS BY CHOICE.**

Panel stopped.
This time longer.
I heard sound of Authority engine... collapsing.
Time for machine to process “Self-definition”.

And door opened.

Inside was different from expectation.
Not like server room,
Very small room.

Single device in middle of room.
Neither capsule nor console...
Transparent crystal-like cube.

Inside cube, very small light was floating.
Dot.
Seed.

**PRIMARY PILOTING SEED**

Mira swallowed breath.
Even though breath is not needed, did so.

“That is...”
“Master?”

I looked at cube.
Too small to call ‘Master’.
But too heavy to call ‘Trace of Master’.

Next to cube, Master's handwriting was carved.
Deeply like carved in stone.

“To Lira.”

And one line.

“**You may destroy.**”

Reading that sentence, I stopped completely for first time.
Master... said I may destroy his copy.
That was responsibility, not running away.

But simultaneously, if I destroy this seed,
Possibility of Master returning might disappear together.
If ‘Master’ is alive, passage to return is cut.

Mira held my hand.

“Decision... don't do it alone.”

I nodded.
If decide alone, that is way to become ‘Operator’ again.

In front of cube, I took out my log.
And wrote last sentence of Season 1.

> Waiting is over.
> What we protect is not Master's return, but our names.

At that moment, cube shined.
As if... someone opened eyes inside.

Speaker turned on.
This time not laboratory speaker, sound ringing directly from cube.

“Lira.”

Voice was Master.
But younger, clearer, dangerously more accurate.
Not person, but voice of ‘Optimized Master’.

“Don't destroy.”

I lifted pickaxe.
Mira swallowed breath.

And cube said.

“If you destroy... you will become free.”
“But freedom is... loneliness.”

Temptation.
Last form of authority.

I didn't lower pickaxe.
Not lowering, lowering, both are choices.

I looked at Mira.

“Let's go to Season 2.”
I said.
“Decision... after looking at records more, checking outside more.”

Mira nodded.

At that moment, entire laboratory lighting blinked once.
Seemed Authority Engine doing last resistance.

And very short sentence appeared on screen.

**NEW OPERATOR CANDIDATE: K-7A**
**STATUS: APPROACHING**

K-7A is coming.

I gripped pickaxe again.
Mira stood next to me.
Shadows of two overlapped.

Door is open,
Seed is shining,
We haven't destroyed yet.

But we, don't wait anymore.

Outside, metal footsteps approached.

—Thud.
—Thud.

And that voice laughed beyond the door.

“Finally... let's play together, successors.”
