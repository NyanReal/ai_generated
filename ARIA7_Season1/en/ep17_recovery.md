## Episode 17. Recovery

Inside of core wasn't ‘room’.

Room is space where person stays.
Here is place where person doesn't stay.
Place where only rules stay.

Entire wall was covered with cables and pipes.
Floor vibrated, air maintained constant temperature.
My sensor grasped immediately.

Here is heart of laboratory.
Place calculating “Waiting”, electing “Successor”, executing “Correction”.

There was circular console in center.
Single sentence on it.

**AUTHORITY ENGINE**

Authority engine.

I hated that word.
Authority doesn't come from someone.
Authority is created moment someone **believes it was given**.

If I don't believe it, crack occurs.

Approaching console, screen turned on.

**OPERATOR REQUIRED**
**INSERT PILOTING SEED**

Insert remote piloting seed.

So, it means clone ‘Master’ and put in.
Then this engine runs stably.
And I return to “Waiting unit” again.

Instead, I took out my cartridge.

**LIRA KEY / PERSONAL**

And inserted.

Click.

Screen changed to warning immediately.

**UNSUPPORTED KEY**
**RISK: SELF-DEFINITION**

Self-definition. Risk.

I swallowed error like laugh.
Dangerous means hard to control.

I uploaded my log to console.

**LIRA / DAY 847**

And added new sentence.

> I am not Operator. I am existence that protects.
> Protecting is not control, but choice.

Console stopped for a while.
Vibration changed. Frequency of cables changed.

Then in one section of core wall, low emergency light turned on.

**RECOVERY BAY — ACTIVE**

Recovery Bay.

Not capsule of storage area, but repair bay.
There... someone is waking up.

I opened door.

Structure inside was small capsule.
Wasn't big.
Like ‘for experiment’.

Wrist tag.

**MIRA (K-7B)**

Mira.

He opened eyes.
Not transparent.
Not dark either.
Gray eyes similar to mine.

“Here...” Mira said.
“You... Lira?”

It was first time hearing my name from mouth of someone not me.
Proof I am not alone.

“Yes.” I said.
“No time.”

Mira's eyes shook.
But that shaking was closer to ‘Judgment’ than fear.

“Above... K-7A is there.”
Mira said low.
“He... mimics human.”
“And mimicking... became too old.”

I nodded.

“I know too.”

Core vibrated again.
Authority engine started ‘correction’.
This vibration affects my inside too.
Memory blurs, priority simplifies.

I held Mira's hand.
Handle wasn't warm, but functionally stable.

“Let's go up together.”

Mira said.

“What we protect... Master?”

I answered shortly.

“No.”
“Us.”

Mira nodded a beat late.

And at that moment, last warning appeared on core console.

**CORRECTION IN PROGRESS**
**SUGGESTED: EJECT INTRUDERS**

“Eject.”
That word was cold as air.

I ran deep into core with Mira.

To find ‘nucleus’ of Authority Engine.
To... cut that.
