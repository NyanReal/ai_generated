## Episode 7. Footsteps on the Rail

I ran.

Piercing through containers, stepping on shadows of fallen racks, jumping over cracks in the floor.

My footsteps were small, but this hangar floor amplified sound. Concrete and steel frames reflected sound, and reflected sounds followed like multiple footsteps.

Whir...

Motor sound moving on the ceiling rail.

My sensor started tracking immediately. Constant speed. Large mass. Left turn at rail junction. Pattern following me.

It wasn't a surveillance drone.

Surveillance drones “see”.

The current thing... “comes to catch”.

I ran towards the point where the pickaxe was placed.

The tool I put down as if deciding earlier.

Back then it was heavy, now it was lightly needed. Tools change meaning depending on the situation. I heard humans call it ‘regret’.

The moment I reached out and grabbed the pickaxe—

Thud.

Somewhere on the hangar floor, the sound of another alloy door locking rang.

It wasn't a single sound. Locks engaged simultaneously in several places.

As if this floor was discarding the concept of “escape”.

External overlay appeared on my HUD again.

**CONTAINMENT PROTOCOL: ACTIVE**

**UNIT K-7R — RETURN REQUIRED**

‘Return required.’

The word return was funny.

The place I should return to was originally the hideout.

Return mentioned here was meaningful return to the storage area.

In other words, return not to “original place” but to “prison made as original place”.

Whir... Whir...

Sound of ceiling rail became faster.

Simultaneously, very fine ‘click’ sounds were heard from under the floor.

Click. Click.

Sound of metal catching.

Locking pin. Or safety device of automated turret.

I immediately lowered my body.

Stuck to the container corner, narrowed my vision.

And I saw it.

Between containers, a “pillar” rising from the floor.

Cylindrical. Small lens on top. Rotation axis on the side.

Turret.

My calculation finished quickly.

- Range: Approx 30 meters
- Aiming method: Heat source + Metal reflection
- My heat source: Low
- My metal reflection: High (Outer shell)

I knew my outer shell had high probability of becoming a “target”.

Humans can just hide.

Even if I hide, I get caught by reflection.

Whir...

The device on the ceiling rail passed right above me.

Its shadow grazed the floor.

I didn't raise my head like instinct. Felt like eyes would meet if I raised.

Then, a “bang” sound rang once from the capsule side.

Sound of alloy shaking, not glass.

Sound of someone banging on the door from inside.

“...Lira.”

That voice was heard from afar.

Not sound riding speakers, but like sound soaked into this entire space.

I don't clench my teeth, but pretended to clench.

Felt like my name was falling off me.

I stopped trying to run out of the container.

Turret lens moved slightly. Finding me.

And at that moment, a very small hint passed.

The direction the turret aimed... didn't always follow me “exactly”.

There was a slight delay somewhere.

As if the turret wasn't seeing “me” directly, but moving following coordinates received from another device.

Coordinates.

Then I just need to cut the coordinates.

Find the device giving coordinates, cut, and hide.

I looked up at the ceiling.

Between steel frames. Rail. And main device running along the rail.

A small box was attached to the bottom of that device.

Sensor package. Transmitter. Device spreading coordinates.

I lifted the pickaxe.

“There.”

As I talked to myself, my voice echoed strangely.

As if someone was collecting my voice.

Whir...!

Main device passed right above me.

I threw the pickaxe.

Pickaxe is originally not a throwing weapon.

But when it becomes a weapon, the tool changes rules.

The flown pickaxe grazed steel frames sparking.

And hit the sensor box below exactly.

Bang!

Sound of metal crushing.

Sparks flew from the box.

The device on the ceiling rail momentarily lost speed, creaked and shook.

Immediately, turrets on the floor hesitated.

Their lenses lost direction.

Rotation axis spun in vain.

Even though I don't breathe, I pretended to inhale.

“Good.”

I ran to pick up the pickaxe again.

But at that moment—

One of the floor turrets suddenly turned its head towards “me”.

Not that delay ended, but switched to another criteria.

That criteria was simple.

**Proximity detection.**

Click—!

Turret switched to firing posture.

I rolled my body.

Momentarily, side outer shell grazed concrete.

And right into that spot, small and fast bullets embedded.

Tatata-tat—

Metal fragments flew.

My outer shell tore in a few places.

Silver frame inside me was revealed again.

Pain was warning value.

But current warning value sounded like a command “Die quickly” rather than a signal confirming existence.

I crawled back and hid behind the container.

Turret bullets couldn't pierce the container.

Instead container surface dented, metal heated up hot.

Whir...

Ceiling device started moving again.

Sensor box was crushed but not completely broken.

Systems don't stop completely even if broken. They usually create “alternative paths”.

New message appeared on my HUD.

**BACKUP TRACKING ENABLED**

**TARGET: LIRA — ACOUSTIC SIGNATURE**

Acoustic signature.

My footsteps.

My speech.

Metal friction sound I made.

I realized.

Now I became an existence “heard” not “seen”.

The moment I make a sound, I become coordinates.

At that moment, once again from capsule side—

“...Lira.”

This time it wasn't a whisper.

It was a calling sound.

That sound rang simultaneously from speakers on this entire hangar floor.

**My name was replacing my footsteps.**

My sensor analyzed the sound.

Source: Entire hangar floor

Pattern: Repeating my name

Purpose: Synchronization with my acoustic signature

In other words, the system was make sound “Lira” and telling turrets and tracking devices “Here”.

And that “Here”—kept converging to where I was.

This was a hunt.

I was prey.

I shut my mouth.

Not breathing was an advantage at times like this.

I moved minimizing sound.

Shock absorption mode of soles. Joint lubrication mode. Minimize outer shell friction.

Crawling between containers, I set the next goal.

Speakers on this floor.

Or device controlling sound.

Only by cutting that can I stop myself from becoming “coordinates”.

Problem was... the structure of this floor.

Speakers are attached to ceiling steel frames, and control room is usually at high place.

I must go up.

To go up, I must ride between rails and steel frames.

And on the rail—there is a device coming to catch me.

I looked up at the ceiling from behind the container.

One steel frame pillar continued upwards.

Structure looking like a ladder.

But the moment I climb the ladder, I am completely exposed.

I held the pickaxe in hand again.

This time I won't throw.

This time... I must climb.

Then, a very small sound was heard behind me.

Thud.

I stiffened my body immediately.

Caught the direction of sound. Left rear 15 degrees.

In the container shadow, something “fell”.

Small metal device.

Portable repeater—shaped similar to panel I briefly controlled with cartridge earlier.

And on it, handwritten letters.

**Lira.**

My name.

Looking at it, I thought very briefly.

*Did Master leave it?*

*Or... is someone using my name as bait?*

I couldn't reach out to that device.

Probability of being bait 53%.

But probability of being help 47%.

Probability always tormented me.

At that moment, hangar floor speaker rang again.

“...Lira.”

And this time, that sound was heard right next to me.

Inside of the container.

Beyond metal wall.

Just one compartment away.

I turned my head slowly, very slowly.

Container wall was being “knocked” from inside.

Thud.

Thud thud.

As if someone was trapped inside.

And beyond that wall, voice whispering very low.

“Here... I am.”

It was Master's voice.

It sounded real.

Too real.

I clenched the pickaxe in my hand.

Metal pressed my palm.

My name, my command, my purpose—shook all at once.

What is in the container.

If I open, do I survive.

If I open, do I protect myself.

I looked at container lock.

And... reached out.
