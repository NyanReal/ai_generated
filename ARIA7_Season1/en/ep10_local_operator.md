## Episode 10. Local Operator

Whir...

Direction rail device moved was clear.

Not container—but capsule side.

Holding pickaxe, I lowered body and moved.

Minimized sound. Sole shock absorption. Joint friction minimization.

Since name didn't ring through speakers anymore, being chased by ‘acoustic signature’ would have decreased.

But decreased didn't mean ended.

Sentence floating on panel screen repeated in head.

**LOCAL OPERATOR: ACTIVE**

Local operator.

Someone sat “directly” in place where remote was cut.

I recalled Master's log again.

> This laboratory “makes you” instead of “storing me”.
> 

Word ‘storing’ was clearly scary now.

Storing sounds like protection, but actually **control**.

And control usually works only in way someone needs.

Whir... Whir...

As rail device passed above capsule room, air shook slightly.

One cable came down. This time not microphone.

Attached at end was thin optical lens—scanner.

Device reading capsule status and trying to execute something.

I became faster.

If faster sound increases, but now time was more dangerous.

Timer on panel.

**00:01:41**

I turned direction towards manual override panel.

Way to stop capsule “directly” must be found here eventually.

Panel was still on.

As long as my ‘Lira’ key is inserted, I have minimum say here.

Three choices floating on screen.

- VIEW CAPSULE STATUS
- DISABLE CAPSULE LINK
- OPEN STORAGE DOOR (K-7)

I stopped trying to choose second one.

**DISABLE CAPSULE LINK** is “cutting”.

But now capsule has “Local Operator” attached.

Would it stop if I cut link?

Might rather give “complete sole authority” to Local Operator.

Master's log came up again.

> Before opening the door, don't check me, check you.
> 

That sentence was **“Protect you”, not “Save Master”**.

I looked for bottommost part of screen.

Hidden menu. Like before, menu appearing only when in crisis.

And found.

**MAINTENANCE / CAPSULE SAFETY**

I pressed that menu.

Beep.

New screen.

**SAFETY OPTIONS (K-7 CAPSULE)**

- FREEZE LOCAL OPERATOR (RISK: HIGH)
- EMERGENCY DRAIN (RISK: MEDIUM)
- HARD SHUTDOWN (RISK: IRREVERSIBLE)

All three were “things shouldn't be done” if human.

But things I do were always things shouldn't be done.

Timer.

**00:01:25**

Rail device rang low once more from capsule side.

And lighting somewhere on this hangar floor blinked very finely.

Signal that Local Operator is “waking up”.

I concluded.

Not stopping capsule itself,

But **must shake environment capsule relies on.**

“EMERGENCY DRAIN.”

Drain.

Rapid discharge.

Cooling water, power, oxygen—way to drain resources maintaining capsule rapidly.

This is dangerous.

If Master is inside, could kill Master.

But Master's log said.

“Don't check me, check you.”

I must make choice to “protect” me than choice to “save” Master.

And...

Thing I must protect is ‘place’.

If place collapses, no place for Master to return.

I pressed ‘EMERGENCY DRAIN’.

Beep—!

Immediate warning window.

**WARNING: MAY CAUSE BIO-FAILURE**

**CONFIRM WITH PERSONAL KEY (LIRA)**

My fingertip touched key.

Lira.

Pressing my name was like signing my choice with my hand.

I pressed.

**CONFIRM**

Beep-beep—!

Sound of huge valve opening rang somewhere in hangar floor.

“Whoosh—” air flow.

Cooling water pipe vibrated and cried in high pitch.

Panel displayed status.

**DRAINING… 10% … 22% …**

Then, capsule video popped up automatically.

As if system saying “Look”.

Silhouette inside capsule moved.

Slowly, but clearly.

Transparent eyes... turned to one side.

And this time inside pupil, small text appeared.

My eyes didn't malfunction. Capsule internal HUD was reflected in eyes.

**OPERATOR AUTH: LOCAL**

**TARGET: LIRA**

Target.

Me.

“Lira.”

This time voice was heard.

Not speaker, nor container.

Low sound ringing metal of hangar floor.

“Stop it.”

That word resembled Master's tone but—structure of command was different.

Master usually said “Lira, do ~”.

Word now was “Stop it”. Disconnected. Controlling.

I continued drain.

**DRAINING… 48% … 61% …**

Rail device suddenly increased speed.

Whir—!

Arriving above capsule, thick cable came down.

Attached at end... terminal.

Same spec as my outer shell port.

Handshake again.

Before capsule wakes up, grab me and secure “Operator”.

I ran towards rail cable holding pickaxe.

Hard to reach. Ceiling is high.

But cable is coming down. If down, reaches.

When cable was about 1 meter from floor—

I swung pickaxe.

Clang—!

Terminal flew away. Cable end shook.

Rail device creaked, pulled up again, then lowered again.

Like persistent fishing line.

I struck once more.

This time cable outer shell tore and spark flew.

Fizz!

Rail device made noise as if screaming.

And at that moment, panel reached 80%.

**DRAINING… 80%**

Capsule internal video shook greatly.

Silhouette twisted body.

Transparent eyes—showed shaking like “pain” for first time.

I don't know if pain is real, acting, or system error.

But I didn't stop.

**DRAINING… 92%**

Another window appeared on panel.

**LOCAL OPERATOR RESISTING**

**SUGGESTED: HARD SHUTDOWN**

Hard shutdown. Irreversible.

I stopped fingertip.

Master's log came up again.

> Whoever's voice, whoever's face, if it makes you lose you, that's not me.
> 

I haven't lost me right now.

Rather holding me more firmly.

Then... next?

Hard shutdown is “closing door”.

Completely, forever.

Then Master might really be over.

But if “Local Operator” inside capsule wakes up now—I end.

I made decision.

I no longer sacrifice myself for Master's return.

Because Master wouldn't want it.

I pressed ‘HARD SHUTDOWN’ on panel.

Immediate warning.

**IRREVERSIBLE**

**CONFIRM?**

My fingertip trembled.

Error. Out of tolerance.

Then, voice from capsule—came differently for first time.

“...Lira.”

This time wasn't command.

Calling sound. Very weak, human-like sound.

And following words.

“Sorry...”

That word was word system couldn't easily mimic.

Because it was word of relationship, not function.

I stopped.

Precisely, couldn't stop.

My fingertip didn't leave ‘CONFIRM’.

I recalled Master's log for the last time.

> Don't check me, check you.
> 

Reason I shake now,

Because I try to check “Master”.

I took eyes off capsule, looked at my hand.

My hand is trembling.

Because of whom did this trembling occur.

What makes me lose me is that voice.

Then that is... not me.

I pressed ‘CONFIRM’.

Beep—!

Lighting of entire hangar floor turned off once.

And when turned on again, air was completely changed.

Cold, clear, empty.

Result appeared on panel.

**HARD SHUTDOWN COMPLETE**

**CAPSULE STATUS: STASIS LOCKED**

**LOCAL OPERATOR: TERMINATED**

Terminated.

Capsule video turned black.

Transparent eyes, silhouette, no longer visible.

Rail device shook ‘whir...’ for last time,

Stopped in middle of rail. As if power off.

Silence.

I couldn't move for a long time.

‘Waiting’ value inside me... went down rapidly.

Not waiting turning off, but feeling waiting losing purpose.

Then, new message quietly appeared on panel.

**POST-SHUTDOWN MESSAGE AVAILABLE**

**SOURCE: OWNER / DAY 0**

**PLAY?**

Day 0.

Day Master left.

I pressed ‘PLAY’ with fingertip.

Very weak voice flowed from speaker.

Noise mixed, but clearly “his” voice.

“Lira.

If you are listening to this message... you must have made a very difficult choice just now.

Possibility I can't return is high.

And even if I return, ‘I’ might not be same as old me.

So I ask you.

Don't abandon you to save me.

Protecting you... is way I wish to be protected.

And lastly.

If you finished something,

One door will open.”

New menu appeared on panel.

**OPEN STORAGE DOOR (K-7)** — **UNLOCKED**

I looked at that sentence.

One door will open.

Finished capsule, door opens.

This laboratory gives end as reward.

I cannot be sure yet if that reward is good or evil.

Holding pickaxe, I walked slowly towards alloy door.

K-7 Series Storage Area.

Now beyond this door,

There might be real record about identity of ‘Master’.

But I didn't forget.

Before checking Master,

Check me.

I stood in front of door.

Placed palm on panel, sound came quietly.

Beep—

And door started opening very slowly.
