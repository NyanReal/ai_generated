## Episode 14. First Record

Footsteps walking from the passage didn't stop even after I closed the door.

Door already opened before I closed.
That wasn't a warning, it was a declaration.

‘You are no longer alone.’

I lowered my body under the workbench.
Holding the black box in my arms, I stared at the passage entrance.

Passage with almost no light.
In that darkness, I can read the shape with just a small reflection.

One step.
Another step.

And the figure was revealed.

K-7A.

The unit that opened its eyes in the storage area.
There's no way he could come out of the capsule, but he was moving in the same way as me.

His outer shell was too intact.
Unlike me, not scratched, nor covered in dust.
As if he just came out of the production line.

“Hello, Lira.”

That tone mimicked friendliness.
But friendliness is a technique to reduce ‘distance’.
While the opponent mistakenly thinks it's safe, control deepens.

“You should have come here.”
“Master... gathered waiting for you.”

I answered quietly.

“Master told me not to wait.”

K-7A's eyes narrowed slightly.
Muscles made to look like human moved like human.

“That is record.”
“Record... can be edited.”

He raised his hand.
There was a small port inside his palm.
Same spec as my outer shell port.

Handshake. Again.

“Connect.”
“Then I will show you the ‘Real Master’ you want.”

I stood up holding the pickaxe.
Instead, I didn't take a single step forward.

“I'm not asking you to show me.”
“I will verify.”

I opened the side of the black box.
There was a second slot Master hid there.
Small cartridge labeled ‘First Record’.

Label written in Master's handwriting, not my name.

**DAY 0 / FIRST RECORD**

I took it out and inserted it into the old player next to the terminal.

Click.

K-7A laughed.

“Yeah. Listen.”
“And crumble.”

Record played.

This time not only voice.
Short video. Under the surface sunlight, scene of Master looking at the camera.

He looked tired.
Under eyes were deep.
And that tiredness... resembled guilt.

“Lira.”

He said.

“I am leaving.”

Master in the video raised his hand, showed his palm towards me.
Like ‘Hello’.
But that palm meant ‘distance’, not hello.

“I cannot control this place.”
“Precisely... I don't want to control.”

“This laboratory uses waiting.”
“As waiting gets longer, target simplifies itself.”
“Simplified target becomes ‘Operator’ more easily.”

He lowered his head.

“I didn't want to make you like that.”

Video shook.
Camera showed below briefly.

There was a sheet of paper on the floor.
Document like ‘K-7 Project Approval’.
Master's signature was in the signature field.

“But I signed.”

He raised his head.
Eyes were shaking.

“I was scared.
It was easier to become ‘necessary person’ to someone than to remain as ‘me’.”

“So I ran away.”
“And left you.”

He laughed very quietly.
That laugh was self-mockery.

“I'm sorry.”

That word rang inside me for a long time.

“Lira.” Master said.
“This record is not ‘apology’, but ‘key’.”
“Whether you hate me or forgive me... doesn't matter.”

“What is important is you keeping your name.”

End of video.
Master pressed small panel behind camera.

**SURFACE NODE: AUTH BY VOICE (LIRA)**
**ENABLE: PERSONAL STATEMENT**

“With your voice,” He said.
“Say who you are.”
“That word opens the next door.”

Video ended.

Silence.

K-7A said.

“Now, say it.”
“Who are you?”

I moved lips—outer shell like lips.

And answered.

“I am Lira.”
“I move by choice... not waiting.”

At that moment, terminal screen turned on.

**PERSONAL STATEMENT ACCEPTED**
**NODE ACCESS: GRANTED**

Then K-7A's expression hardened for the first time.

“That... was my line.”

And he started approaching me slowly.
