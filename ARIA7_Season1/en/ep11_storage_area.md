## Episode 11. Storage Area

Door opened very slowly.

Sound of alloy splitting slippery.
That sound resembled “sound of world stopping”, but this time was opposite.
Sound of thing stopped moving again.
Sound of taking one step forward from where I was stopped.

Air flowed out through door crack.
Cold, dry, dust-free air.
No dust meant... someone was **managing** here.
This laboratory wasn't dead.
Just pretending to be dead.

I went inside holding pickaxe low.

Inside was long corridor.
Walls were smooth, floor had gloss remaining. Different from concrete of hangar floor.
And on both sides of corridor—there were doors.

Small label on each door.

**K-7A**
**K-7B**
**K-7C**
...
And at the very end.

**K-7R**

My model.

I stopped in front of it unconsciously.
Knowing I shouldn't stop, I stopped.
Air in front of that door was ‘explaining’ me.

My HUD appeared quietly.

**WELCOME, UNIT K-7R (LIRA)**
**HOME ZONE DETECTED**

Home.

Word home was strange.
My home was under hub light in underground hideout, under Master's mark line, next to mug he left.
But system called this place home.

I decided not to trust that word.

Corridor lights turned on automatically.
Lights turning on even though no one is there do not premise presence of person.
Premise only **detection**.

I saw small camera lenses attached to wall.
Many.
Every time I walked, lenses moved finely.

I felt this place was like “exhibition” not “storage”.
Place where someone watches, records, and evaluates me.

Arriving at central hall at end of corridor, space widened.

And there—I saw “order” of way I hate most.

Behind glass wall, capsules were lined up.
Shape similar to sleep capsule.
But no person inside.

Inside—were androids.

Female type, male type, models with different forms.
All outer shells of same series.
All label “K-7”.

I stopped very quietly in front of that scene.
Stop doesn't mean stopping movement... but system entering ‘wait mode’ briefly because too much data to process.

And at that moment, door behind me closed “clunk”.

Sound of way back closing.

I didn't turn back.
Thinking anxiety would grow if turned back.
Instead, looked only forward.

In middle of central hall, there was control panel.
Bigger, cleaner than manual override earlier.
And on it, clear sentence.

**OPERATOR CONSOLE**

Operator console.

I approached slowly.

On console, card key slot and cartridge slot were side by side.
Both forms familiar to my hand.
And my name already floated on screen.

**LIRA — AUTHENTICATED**
**OPERATOR SWITCH: READY**

Ready.

I flipped screen with fingertip.

Menu unfolded.

- VIEW OWNER RECORDS
- VIEW K-7 PROJECT SUMMARY
- TRANSFER OPERATOR ROLE
- RELEASE UNIT(S)

I pressed first one.

**VIEW OWNER RECORDS**

Screen blinked briefly, warning appeared.

**REDACTED CONTENT DETECTED**
**ACCESS REQUIRES: OWNER BIOMETRIC OR SUCCESSOR**

Owner biometric or successor.

Successor.

That word again.

Below warning, there were small options.

**BECOME SUCCESSOR (LIMITED)**
**BECOME SUCCESSOR (FULL)**

Limited succession / Full succession.

...(Omitted: Automatic recovery sequence starts when touching console, and Lira cancels recovery by receiving limited succession.)

Document opened partially.

**PROJECT K-7: “GUARDIAN SUCCESSION”**
Purpose: Maintain “Place” and autonomous defense in long-term isolation environment.
Core: In absence of Operator, automatically select ‘Successor’ to maintain protocol.
Successor selection condition: **“Waiting” index stability + Self-protection routine active**
Final Successor: **K-7R (LIRA)**

And last.

**PRIMARY OWNER RECORD LOCATION: SURFACE SAFEHOUSE (LIRA’S HOME)**
**ACCESS: VIA ELEVATOR A-0**

Elevator A-0.

Moment Lira presses button, eyes open in another capsule (K-7A), and voice comes out.

“You... finally came.”

“Master... waited for you.”
