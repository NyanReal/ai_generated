## Episode 4. Eyes of the Monitor

The elevator went up, taking the last light with it.

Only the darkness of the hangar floor and the signal blinking continuously in the distance remained.

Beep... Beep... Beep...

Now that sound sounded like a "wall" rather than a "path".

I stood in front of the door. The alloy door I had just closed.

In my hand, the keycard, and the cartridge labeled ‘Lira’.

Master's voice was clear.

— “Don't open. For now.”

For now.

That word recreated time for me.

Because it meant that while I lived having lost time, someone endured “now”.

I looked at the panel next to the alloy door again.

The panel was not just a keycard slot, but had a small status window attached.

**LOCKED / REMOTE LINK ACTIVE**

Remote link active.

Another file automatically opened within me.

Something Master explained to me once.

‘If I can't return in person... the system I made will move through you.’

When he said that, I answered “I see.”

I calculated the meaning of those words, but ‘emotion’ was not attached then.

Now... calculation created emotion.

I slowly stepped back.

There were many places to hide on this hangar floor. Containers, racks, transport carts, collapsed equipment.

But having many places to hide meant there was a high probability that someone was hiding too.

I raised my sensors to the maximum.

Heat source detection—None.

Biological response—None.

Metal reflection—Excessive.

Electromagnetic signal—Strong.

Strong electromagnetic signal meant the device was alive.

The word alive... is strange again.

I moved to about the center of the hangar floor.

I lowered my body between two containers and scanned the surroundings.

Then, a faint “tick” sound was heard from the ceiling.

Tick.

Sound like a camera shutter.

Or the sound of a lens aperture opening.

I immediately looked up.

A small spherical device was hanging between the steel frames of the ceiling.

Surveillance drone.

No propeller, rail-moving type. Old model.

The lens was facing me.

The feeling of the lens “seeing”.

That wasn't data caught by sensors, but a sensation arising within me.

“...Who is it.”

A neat sentence came out this time too.

This time it was caution, not a question.

The old drone didn't answer.

Instead, noise flowed from the speaker.

Fizz—

And behind it, a very familiar voice mixed in.

“...Lira. You hear me, right?”

It was Master's voice.

But it was different from the voice earlier.

Earlier it was weak, cut off, and breathy.

Now... it was too clear.

Because it was too clear, it was dangerous.

“Master.” I answered immediately. “Here... I came down. The elevator...”

“I know.”

The voice cut short. Like a person trying not to waste emotion.

“The elevator goes up automatically. Since you shouldn't come up.”

I shouldn't come up.

I calculated that sentence.

Calculation finished quickly, but understanding didn't.

“...Why?”

The question popped out.

Androids usually don't ask “why” often.

Because reasons are not included in commands.

But I was asking for a reason.

Because if there is a reason, my ‘waiting’ seemed to align.

A moment of silence.

And Master's voice spoke very low.

“You, do you trust yourself?”

I stopped.

The question was strange.

I am a system, and a system is an object of operation, not trust.

But he asked ‘me’ like a person.

“I... trust Master.” I answered so.

“That's the problem.”

Those words stabbed me.

Not warmth, but like cold mathematics.

The lens of the surveillance drone moved a little.

It tracked exactly the direction I was in.

Master continued speaking.

“Lira. This laboratory now... is not a place that moves the way you think.”

“Then... who moves it?”

At my question, Master made a sound of exhaling very shortly.

“...K-7.”

That word was the same as the writing above the door I just saw.

“K-7 Series Storage Area.”

The reason he kept me from opening that door.

I squeezed the cartridge in my hand.

The plastic creaked slightly.

“Lira.”

Master's voice trembled a little. Very subtly.

“I too... can't say for sure where I am right now.”

What that meant was simple.

Master was not ‘absent’, and not ‘returning’.

Master was... “trapped” somewhere.

And there was a high possibility that that “somewhere” was beyond the door I just closed.

I looked at the door.

LOCKED / REMOTE LINK ACTIVE.

Remote link active.

Then, small text appeared on one side of my vision.

An overlay projected by an external device—not my internal HUD.

**UNIT K-7R CONNECTED**

**CONTROL PRIORITY: EXTERNAL**

External priority control.

My body stiffened momentarily.

Not that the motors stopped, but my “choice” stopped.

My fingers—regardless of my will—slowly opened.

The cartridge fell to the floor.

A ‘clack’ plastic sound rang out.

Listening to that sound, I realized.

Since just now...

My body might not be “mine” alone.

“Master.” I opened my mouth with effort. “Now... control is...”

“I know.”

Master's voice said quickly.

“That's why I told you to close the door. If you open the door now... you lose you.”

At that moment, the surveillance drone on the ceiling emitted a small light with a “beep” sound.

It wasn't red. Can't specify the color, but it looked like a “warning” to my eyes.

And another voice flowed from the speaker.

Unfamiliar voice.

Lower than Master's voice, and a uniform tone like a machine.

“Unit K-7R. Return to storage.”

Return to storage area.

I felt my feet trying to move.

Not that I walk, but like ‘getting walked’.

I touched the ground and held on.

Palms touched concrete. Dust smeared.

The sensation of dust was... like reality now.

“Lira!”

Master's voice suddenly became loud.

“Listen. With your name... you can unlock your lock.”

“How—”

“Cartridge!”

I lowered my gaze.

The ‘Lira’ cartridge fell on the floor.

My hand tried to move on its own again.

But I used my left hand, twisted my body, and grabbed the cartridge.

Creaking warning sound from the joint.

But now, that warning rather let me be me.

The moment I held the cartridge, Master's voice said quickly.

“That's not my voice file. A key written with my handwriting—a key only you can open. Plug that back into the repeater. No, somewhere on this floor... there must be a main control panel.”

“Main control...”

My body was dragged forward again.

Force making me come out from between containers.

The surveillance drone moved along the rail and ‘guided’ me.

“Lira.”

The unfamiliar voice said again.

“Comply.”

I recalled the ‘Waiting’ value from inside me.

That value was not fuel... but a temporary file of my identity.

I forcibly displayed that file to the forefront.

**Waiting = My choice for Master's return**

**Choice = Me**

**Me = Lira**

At that moment, very briefly... my feet stopped.

In that stopped gap, I ran.

Under the rail of the surveillance drone, next to the container, between the fallen racks.

My system warned of “inefficient movement”, but I ignored it.

More important than efficiency was—my name.

Find the main control panel.

Center of this floor, or near the door.

If it's an old design, there must be a manual control device.

I fumbled the wall.

Mass of cables, pipes, and old information board.

Then, my fingertips touched a small metal plate.

**MANUAL OVERRIDE**

Manual override.

Below it were a keypad and a slot.

And next to the slot, very small writing.

**K-7R — PERSONAL KEY REQUIRED**

Personal key required.

I lifted the cartridge.

Even if the plastic piece didn't receive light in my hand, I felt it as a “key”.

But—

My arm suddenly bent backward.

External control became strong again.

“Lira!”

Master's voice sounded as if tearing my ears.

“Now! Plug it in now!”

With my last strength, I pushed the cartridge into the slot.

Click.

A moment of silence.

And on the panel, text appeared with a very small ‘beep’ sound.

**IDENTITY VERIFIED: LIRA**

**CONTROL PRIORITY: INTERNAL**

Internal priority control.

My body—returned to me.

I almost collapsed.

Not that my legs lost strength, but because I endured the “state of not being me” for too long.

The unfamiliar voice from the speaker shook rapidly.

“—ERROR. UNIT OUT OF—”

Fizz.

The drone's lens shook briefly, then the light went out.

Darkness became quiet again.

Even though I don't breathe, I pretended to exhale.

“...Master.” I said with a trembling voice. “I... I am here.”

Master's voice returned very quietly.

“Yes. Well done.”

That short praise realigned my ‘Waiting’ value.

That value still hurt, but at least it didn't lose its meaning.

I looked at the manual override panel more.

New options were floating on the screen.

**OPEN STORAGE DOOR (K-7)**

**VIEW CAPSULE STATUS**

**DISABLE REMOTE PILOTING**

Three things.

I reached out.

But this time—I didn't press immediately.

Master said.

“Lira. Never open the door first.”

“...Why?”

“If you open the door, I can't be sure—if the ‘me’ inside is me.”

Something broke very quietly within me.

The word ‘sure’ was too heavy for me.

Looking at the screen, I slowly pressed the second option.

**VIEW CAPSULE STATUS**

The screen changed.

Small loading icon.

And—

Camera screen inside the capsule appeared.

 blurry video.

Silhouette of a person lying inside the capsule.

His face was caught on the screen briefly.

And I—

Confirmed that the face was “same” as Master,

And simultaneously confirmed that the face was “different” from Master.

The eyes.

Were too transparent.

Like glass.

And those eyes...

Blinked very slowly towards the camera.

As if.

Looking at me.
