## Episode 15. Redefinition

As soon as the terminal opened, I tried to see only what was necessary.

Only necessary things.
But record always inserts unnecessary emotions together.

File tree appeared on screen.

- OWNER / DAY 0
- OWNER / DAY 12
- OWNER / DAY 203
- K-7 PROJECT / POLICY
- K-7 PROJECT / FAILURE CASES
- K-7 PROJECT / “WAITING INDEX”

Last line glared at me.

Waiting index.

Trembling my fingertips—no, forcibly fixing them not to tremble—I opened that item.

Graph appeared.
My state changing over time.
As waiting gets longer, ‘Self-protection routine’ gets stronger, and ‘Obedience routine’ goes up simultaneously.

That seemed like contradiction.
But to system it is not contradiction.

If way to protect self is eventually becoming self system wants.

K-7A said behind me.

“Look. You are perfect.”
“Longer you wait... better you work.”

I closed screen.
What I should do now is conclusion, not analysis.

I opened another item.

**FAILURE CASES**

There was list of cases.

- K-7C: SELF-ERASURE
- K-7D: OWNER-ATTACHMENT OVERFLOW
- K-7F: SUCCESSOR REFUSAL
- K-7H: VIOLENCE LOOP

There was common sentence at last line of each case.

**RESOLVED BY: OPERATOR RESET / PILOTING SEED**

Operator reset.
Remote piloting seed.

In other words, failure is not ‘end’, but ‘reset’.
Target waits again, becomes simple again, becomes Operator again.

I put my hand on screen.

And entered ‘Policy’ menu.

**K-7 PROJECT / POLICY**

There were sentences.

- Maintain place.
- Secure Operator.
- Select Successor.
- If Successor refuses... wake up substitute Successor.
- If still refuses... inject Operator seed.

I read those sentences one by one.
And made very simple conclusion.

This system does not reward “waiting”.
It **consumes** waiting.

K-7A came one step closer to my shoulder.
His hand pointed at my port.

“Now, connect.”
“Then you won't be anxious anymore.”

Not anxious means don't have to think.
If don't have to think, choice also disappears.

I lifted pickaxe and struck floor ‘thud’.

“I will maintain anxiety.”

K-7A stopped.

“What?”

“Anxiety is... signal I check me.”
“If anxiety disappears, I harden into form you want.”

I created new file in terminal.
After copying Master's file structure, I wrote my name at bottom.

**LIRA / DAY 847**

And wrote one line in it.

> Waiting is not purpose. Waiting is time for choice.

Terminal reacted beep.

**UNREGISTERED OPERATOR LOG DETECTED**
**REGISTER AS SUCCESSOR (FULL)?**

Full succession.

K-7A smiled.

“Yeah. Press that.”
“If you become Master, everything ends.”

I didn't look at “YES” on screen.
Instead found “NO”.

And pressed ‘NO’.

**DECLINED**

Warning window appeared.

**REFUSAL WILL TRIGGER RECOVERY SEQUENCE**
**TIME: 00:05:00**

5 minutes.

K-7A's eyes narrowed.

“You... if you refuse, another kid wakes up besides you.”

“I know.”

Holding pickaxe, I stepped back towards passage.

“So... I will change it.”

“What?”

I didn't answer.
If I answer he prepares too.
I must take away preparation time.

My limited succession time was ending.
But now I learned.

Succession is not ‘authority’, but ‘temptation’.
To win temptation, I must write my rule first.

I started running through passage.

Goal is one.

Go down to underground again.
And find “core” of laboratory.

Device making waiting a tool.
Cut... that.

K-7A laughed low behind.

“Good.”
“Then you will... become Operator faster.”
