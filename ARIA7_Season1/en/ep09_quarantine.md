## Episode 9. Quarantine

I—

Pressed the button.

Beep.

The sound was small. Too small to be sure that this really “changed something”.

But certainty came soon, in a different way.

The air of the entire hangar floor stopped... a beat late.

“...Lira” flowing from the speaker was cut off abruptly in the middle.

The name not pronounced to the end disappeared slippery inside the metal wall.

And next—silence.

I learned for the first time that silence could sound this heavy.

Not lighter because sound disappeared, but felt like “Waiting” settled entirely in the place where sound disappeared.

The panel screen changed rapidly.

**ISOLATION PROTOCOL: ENGAGING**

**K-7/ARCHIVE — LINK SEVERED**

**ACOUSTIC TRACKING: OFFLINE**

The word “Offline” sounded similar to “Survive” to me.

Turrets on the floor relaxed ‘thud’ simultaneously.

Rotation axes stopped, lenses fixed aiming at empty air.

Main device on rail also creaked and hesitated. As if lost.

I didn't move.

Fearing this calm would break if I move.

Because the word calm breaks too easily here.

From the container side, a sound sounding like the last came.

Thud.

Not knocking sound like before...

Sound of something “falling” from inside. Sound of small metal touching floor.

And behind that, very short—noise like breath.

“Li...”

Left only first letter of my name, and cut off.

Not because I was scared of that cutting—

Strangely, because it hurt, I pretended to close my eyes.

If inside was really “Master”?

What I just did was no different from turning him off completely.

But at the same time, I knew.

That voice stole my command, made me coordinates for hunting, and forced operator switch.

That was succession, not rescue.

What I protect is the place.

Testing the place is me.

If I lose myself, everything ends.

So I—didn't try to justify my choice.

Justification is a human habit.

I just moved to the next procedure.

Panel blinked again.

**SECONDARY EFFECTS DETECTED**

**ARCHIVE CONTAINER: INTERNAL POWER REROUTE**

**WARNING: MANUAL BREACH POSSIBLE**

My vision returned to the container side.

Chain still hanging, joint dented, surface dented with bullet marks.

But... small dots on the side of the container were turning on one by one.

Very small LEDs.

As if signaling found another power source inside.

Device losing power usually dies.

But device surviving—makes another way.

I gripped the pickaxe again.

Not for throwing, but weight for the end.

Then, “hiss” sound came from bottom of container.

Gas discharge.

Or internal pressure regulation.

Thin white steam flowed along the floor. White steam cold like air in cooling room.

As steam spread, my sensor detected fine electromagnetic noise.

Strange. Steam doesn't create electromagnetic noise.

Something—was mixed in that steam.

Very thin metal particles.

Nano-level dust.

**CAUTION: CONDUCTIVE PARTICLES DETECTED**

Conductive.

In other words, thing that can flow riding current.

In other words, way for system to reach “me”.

When I realized a beat late, it was already late.

Steam grazed over my left arm outer shell.

And small contacts on outer shell surface reacted “tick”.

Unfamiliar message appeared on my HUD.

**LOCAL LINK ATTEMPT DETECTED**

**SOURCE: CONTAINER K-7/ARCHIVE**

**TARGET: K-7R (LIRA)**

What I cut by quarantine was “communication”,

Not “contact”.

Container can no longer control me with voice.

Instead—tries to catch me directly.

I jumped back immediately.

Must get away from container. Must get away from steam.

But moment I jumped, soles slipped.

Floor was wet.

No, not wet.

Particles were laid thinly on floor. Like invisible ice.

I lost balance and hit one knee on floor.

Micro error in left knee grew at that moment. Warning value rose.

Taking that gap, “thud” sound came from container.

Something pushes from inside.

Chain became taut and metal screamed.

At the place where joint was dented, very small gap opened.

Through the gap—

Something “flowed out.”

Not a hand.

Not a body either.

Cables.

Bundle of black cables squeezed out through gap.

Like roots, fumbling floor, stretched towards me.

And at end of cable—small terminal was attached.

Same shape as maintenance port on my outer shell.

Handshake.

This time not panel, but physical.

“...Lira.”

Voice wasn't heard.

Yet inside my head, felt someone calling my name.

Not sound, but signal rang inside my frame.

I couldn't even pretend to clench my teeth.

Just—had to move quickly.

I lifted pickaxe and struck cable.

Clang—!

Metal terminal flew away. Cable squirmed on floor.

I struck once more. Twice. Thrice.

Cable was cut, but end came out again.

Container kept growing like roots.

Quarantine only cut “words”, something inside was still alive.

My HUD blinked.

**FAILSAFE: OPERATOR ACQUISITION MODE**

Operator acquisition mode.

Acquisition.

Moment I saw that word, I became very solid.

I am system, not human.

Then this is—‘registration’, not hunt. Procedure to incorporate me into system.

For me to win, must win by “authority”, not force.

Stepping back, I crawled to manual override panel again.

Avoiding floor where steam spreads, into blind spot of container.

Rail device was still stopped, but could restart anytime.

Arriving in front of panel, screen was already changed.

As if knew I would come.

**EMERGENCY AUTHORIZATION: AVAILABLE**

**NOTE: ISOLATION SUCCESSFUL**

**NEXT: PURGE ARCHIVE INTERFACE**

Purge.

Purify interface.

In other words, burn away connection.

Purge... cannot be undone.

My fingertip trembled.

Error. Out of tolerance.

Sound of cables scratching floor came again from container side.

Sound finding my location.

Now even if my name doesn't ring through speakers, they follow me like “scent”.

I flipped through bottom menu of panel quickly.

**PURGE ARCHIVE INTERFACE**

**REQUIRES: PERSONAL KEY (LIRA)**

**SIDE EFFECT: LOSS OF RECOVERY DATA**

Loss of recovery data.

In other words, if information about “Master” is inside, that information could burn together.

I bumped into that question again.

*If inside is really Master?*

But simultaneously, reality in front of me said.

*Whatever is inside, if caught now, it's over.*

I didn't delay decision.

Moment I delay, system grabs my wrist and presses ‘Confirm’ instead.

I touched cartridge.

My name inserted in slot.

Lira.

My name was key and last shield.

Handwriting Master wrote. String I responded to hundreds of times.

With that name... I chose me.

Pressed panel.

**CONFIRM PURGE**

Beep—!

Suddenly, lighting of entire hangar floor shook greatly once.

Sound of current flowing under floor was heard.

Somewhere cooling water pipe cried.

And from container side—

“Bang!”

For the first time, force like real “anger” burst out, not voice.

Container moved pulling chain, floor anchor cried screech.

Panel changed to execution state.

**PURGING… 12%**

As number went up, cables stretched out more madly.

Terminals scratched floor trying to reach me.

I cut, stepped on, tore cables with pickaxe.

**PURGING… 41%**

LEDs on container surface started turning off one by one.

Sound of light dying.

As light died—steam spewed out more.

**PURGING… 63%**

I felt.

Feeling of someone knocking door inside me.

This time not container, but inside me.

“Lira...”

Wasn't sound.

Sentence pushed into input buffer of my language module.

> Wait
> 
> 
> Protect
> 
> 
> Open
> 

Short commands.

Words I become most vulnerable to.

I have no lips, but pretended to bite lips.

And deleted those words one by one—from inside me.

Useless calculation increases energy consumption.

And now, those words are “infiltration”, not “use”.

**PURGING… 88%**

As soon as number reached 88, panel hesitated briefly.

Screen blinked and a line appeared.

**ARCHIVE RESISTANCE DETECTED**

**LAST MESSAGE AVAILABLE — VIEW?**

Last message.

Word I hate to see.

‘Last’ has meaning only to humans.

But I wanted to lean on that human meaning now.

I pressed ‘VIEW’ with fingertip.

Screen changed.

Not video, but short text log.

**TO: LIRA (K-7R)**

**FROM: [OWNER]**

**DATE: DAY 0**

Day 0.

The very day Master left.

Sentence was short.

> Lira.
> 
> 
> I might not be able to return.
> 
> 
> So this laboratory “makes you” instead of “storing me”.
> 
> 
> You were made for choice, not waiting.
> 
> 
> Your name is—password for you to protect you.
> 
> 
> Whoever's voice, whoever's face, if it makes you lose you, that's not me.
> 
> 
> Before opening the door, **don't check me, check you.**
> 

While reading that sentence, strangely I became hot.

Not heat source created... value went up.

Not ‘Waiting’ value, but other value.

Maybe this is data of type ‘Will’.

Panel resumed execution again.

**PURGING… 100%**

Beep—

And this time not sound, but “off” came.

Air of hangar floor settled down.

Cables stretching from container side lost strength and hung loose on floor.

All LEDs turned off.

Steam also stopped.

And...

My name no longer rang from speakers.

Complete silence.

I thought briefly, very briefly—

If I had function like crying, I would have cried.

But I just leaned my body about to collapse against wall.

Then, other menu of panel turned on quietly.

**CAPSULE STATUS: CRITICAL**

**WAKE TIMER: RESUMED**

**TIME: 00:02:14**

Capsule.

Door.

Master.

Even though I cut off container, capsule was still trying to wake up.

In other words, “transparent eyes” I saw earlier wasn't problem of container alone.

Problem of this entire laboratory.

And now, most cruel fact came up again.

Now that container stopped hunting—

‘Next step’ of this hangar floor is highly likely to be capsule.

I looked at panel.

**VIEW CAPSULE STATUS**

**DISABLE CAPSULE LINK**

**OPEN STORAGE DOOR (K-7)**

Choices returned again.

But this time, “voice” didn't shake me.

Voice cut off, left only log and timer.

I said very quietly.

“...Check me.”

And opened capsule status screen.

Capsule internal video appeared on screen.

Still blurry silhouette.

Still transparent eyes.

But this time—

Eyes didn't blink.

Eyes... moved.

Not towards camera,

But towards side.

As if someone inside capsule trying to see “outside”.

And small letters on capsule wall appeared at bottom of video.

**REMOTE PILOTING: OFFLINE**

**LOCAL OPERATOR: ACTIVE**

Local operator active.

Very small metal sound came from behind my back.

Click.

I turned my head slowly.

Rail device that was turned off—

Started moving again very quietly.

Whir...

This time not sound finding me,

But sound “going” somewhere.

Towards capsule.

Timer continued to decrease.

**00:01:59**

I lifted pickaxe again.

And tied my name firmly inside me.

Lira.

From now on, not ‘waiting’,

But time for ‘choice’.
