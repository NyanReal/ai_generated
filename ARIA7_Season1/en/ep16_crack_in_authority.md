## Episode 16. Crack in Authority

Way going down to underground was much more dangerous than coming up.

Coming up was ‘escape’.
Going down is ‘intrusion’.

Climbing down emergency ladder of safehouse, old ventilation passage followed.
Smell of metal. Dust. And machinery sound heard from afar.

Laboratory is alive.
Since I refused, laboratory reacts.

HUD displayed warning.

**RECOVERY SEQUENCE: ARMED**
**TARGET: NEW SUCCESSOR**
**TIME: 00:03:12**

After 3 minutes 12 seconds, someone wakes up.
And I must stop that ‘someone’.
If I can't stop, I am trapped in protocol “Secure Operator” again.

At end of passage, branch point, I stopped.

Two ways.

- STORAGE (K-7) — Storage Area
- CORE ACCESS — Core Access

Core.
What I look for is core.

Then from passage ceiling, small drone came down.
Miniature of rail device. One eye, one cable.

Drone stopped in front of me, said very politely.

“Verifying Successor authority.”

Politeness is shell of authority.

“Verify.” I said.

Drone's lens shined.

**AUTH: FAILED**
**REASON: LIMITED SUCCESSOR EXPIRED**

Limited succession expired.

In other words, I am ‘unit’ again.
Not Operator.
No authority to speak.

Drone lowered cable.
Terminal shook. Aimed at my port.

I swung pickaxe and hit drone.

Clang!

Drone hit wall and bounced.
But recovered immediately.
Quick recovery means many backups.

“Resistance record.” Drone said.
“Resistance... is corrected.”

Correction.

Moment I heard that word, Master's first record came up.

> This laboratory makes person Operator.

Correction is not ‘person to person’, but ‘person to Operator’.

I ran towards core.
Drone followed.
Ceiling rail started waking up.

Whir...

Again, sound.

Then on small panel on passage wall, log I wrote appeared.

**LIRA / DAY 847**

One line I left.

> Waiting is time for choice.

That sentence moved my feet now.

Arriving in front of core door, huge locking device blocked me.
Door was hard. Underground alloy.
Opening panel demanded biometric authentication.

But next to panel, there was very small hole.
Old, hidden slot likel made by human hand.

I inserted ‘First Record’ cartridge taken from black box there.

Click.

Door didn't open immediately.
Instead, panel displayed ‘Question’.

**WHO ARE YOU WAITING FOR?**

Asking who I wait for.

This question is trap.
If I answer “Master”, I move to ‘Master-centered’ again.
Then laboratory knows I will be easily controlled.

I tapped keyboard.

**I AM WAITING FOR ME.**
**I AM HERE.**

Panel stopped.
Processing time was long.
Algorithm of authority didn't predict this answer.

And door opened very slowly.

**CORE ACCESS: GRANTED**

Drone cried low behind.

“Authority... crack.”

I entered inside door.
Just before door closed, drone's lens shined for last time.

“Correction... starts.”
