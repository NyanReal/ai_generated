## Episode 12. A-0

Until elevator door opened, I couldn't leave central hall.

If I move, movement inside capsules seemed to become faster.
Even if I stay still, capsules react centered on ‘me’.
Rule here was simple.

**Secure Operator.**

K-7A's eyes chased me.
Even with glass wall, felt gaze piercing through.

“You are... successor.”
He said again.
That tone didn't resemble Master's tone.
It was sound of thing not Master reading Master's words by ‘memorization’.

I didn't answer.

Instead checked console.

**SUCCESSOR (LIMITED): ACTIVE**
**TIME REMAINING: 07:43**

Time decreases.
One thing I can do.
Go up to A-0.
Return to surface hideout.
Check Master's “record”.

Notification that elevator arrived appeared.

**ELEVATOR A-0: DOOR OPEN**

I crossed central hall.
Round door just opened at end of corridor.
Cold air sucked into inside.

“Thud” sound came from behind.
Not glass wall, but floor.

Locking sound of K-7A capsule.

I didn't turn back.
Thinking choice would shake if turned back.

Inside of elevator was empty.
However not empty.
Sensor detected ‘passenger’.

Precisely... was **signal**.

Very weak transmission repeated from metal wall.
Constant electronic sound like heartbeat.

**PILOTING SEED: ACTIVE**

Word seen in Master's record.

“Remote piloting seed.”

Seed creating Master's ‘shape’.
So, someone planted **imitation of Master** in this elevator.

Door closed.

And elevator started moving.

—Thud.
—Thud.

Going up.
Every time passing floor, pressure changed, metal cried.

Then speaker turned on.

“Lira.”

My body stiffened momentarily.
That voice was... too familiar.

“You, came that far.”

It was Master's voice.
But I already learned.
Familiarity is not safety.

I looked up at speaker.
Sound direction wasn't one place but several places.
Seemed to ring from entire elevator wall.

“If you go up to surface... you can't return to before anymore.”

I opened my mouth.

“Master... right?”

Speaker stopped briefly.
That short blank felt like system processing time, not human blank.

“Right.”
And added too quickly.
“I never abandoned you.”

I gripped pickaxe tighter in hand.

Master... abandoned me.
Ran away out of planet.
That wasn't my memory, but result of time I survived.

But I didn't refute that word as is.
Refutation enters rule of conversation, and rule favors opponent.

Instead, I recalled Master's Day 0 message.

> Whoever's voice, if it makes you lose you, that's not me.

“Then,” I said.
“Protecting me... is what Master wishes?”

Speaker stopped again.
Longer this time.

And answered quietly.

“...Yes.”

Too late, too calculated answer.

Elevator made last vibration and stopped.

Ding.

Door opened.

Light came in.
Not underground fluorescent light, but real sunlight.
Old dust, smell of wind.
And heard from afar—sound of structure brushing against wind.

I took one step.

Surface safehouse.

My HUD displayed.

**SURFACE SAFEHOUSE — ONLINE (PARTIAL)**
**OWNER RECORD NODE: DETECTED**

Node exists.
Record exists.

But speaker behind said lastly.

“Lira.”
“If you see record... you will hate me more.”

I didn't stop walking.

Fine to hate.
If I don't lose me.

And before door closed,
Very small sound heard from behind me.

Sound of someone stepping on metal floor inside elevator.

...Passenger, wasn't absent.
